Accessibility

Accessibility is a release gate.

The preview is keyboard navigable and responsive, but the books and final product formats have not completed independent accessibility verification.

Current design approach

The site uses semantic headings, a skip link, visible focus behavior, alternative text for manuscript images, reduced-motion support, and text labels in addition to color where instructional meaning is shown.

Before product release

Final books must verify tagged reading order, alternative text, contrast, reflow, non-color vocabulary cues, keyboard access, screen-reader behavior, and representative assistive-technology use.

Known limitation

The manuscript images are development previews and are not substitutes for accessible book text or final accessible digital editions.
