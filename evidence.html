Evidence policy

Clear about what each check means.

Prism Words is in development. The books shown on this site are review candidates, not released or externally certified products.

Software checks

Current checks can identify vocabulary assignments, sentence ranges, grammar signals, morphology candidates, lookahead use, and whether required records are present. A passing check means the manuscript followed the tested rule set.

What software does not prove

A software pass does not prove instructional effectiveness, child engagement, accessibility, official leveling, standards mastery, or learning outcomes. Those require independent review and human evidence.

External frameworks

Common Core, Dolch, Fry, Lexile, DRA, and Guided Reading references are kept separate. Internal planning crosswalks are not presented as certification, assessment results, or official publisher levels.

Release standard

Before release, a book must complete curriculum, editorial, human-use, accessibility, rights, and production review in addition to deterministic software validation.
